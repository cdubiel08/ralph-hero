### 3. Clarity — PASS (with 1 minor naming note)

Success criteria are specific and testable. Each phase has `Automated Verification` and `Manual Verification` checklists; the `- [ ]` checkbox format is used consistently. Top-level "Desired End State → Verification" section also enumerates the cross-phase contract.

**Minor naming note (non-blocking)**: Phase 3 places the new merge-gate step at "Step 4b" "immediately after the existing Step 4 review-decision guard". The actual file has Step 4 (review decision) → Step 4a (autonomous merge gate, RALPH_AUTO_MERGE-gated) → Step 5. Placing the Scout-report gate at "4b" puts it alphabetically after 4a, which is the natural reading order. However, the plan's prose says "immediately after Step 4 review-decision guard" — which could ambiguously mean either "between 4 and 4a" or "after 4a". Implementer should place the new gate **after Step 4a** (so reading order is 4 → 4a → 4b → 5), since the Scout-report gate is conceptually a peer of the autonomous merge gate. This is a 30-second decision the implementer can make from context, not a structural defect.

### 4. Scope — PASS

Shared Constraints clarify the boundary with Feature E (outcome recording is automatic when E lands; F no-ops gracefully if E hasn't) and Feature B (taxonomy patch can ship inline per parent plan's integration strategy, paragraph 4).

The single-PR atomic delivery is justified in the "Why grouped" paragraph: SOUL → trigger comment → merge gate → schedule all share the Scouts team contract, and the taxonomy edit is small enough to ship inline.
