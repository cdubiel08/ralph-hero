- **[Verification quality] The Phase 1 prefix-identity regression test was over-specified and would fail on a natural fixture.** As originally written, Phase 1 asserted `enumerateDirections(...).slice(0, config.limit)` deep-equals `rankDirections(...)` byte-for-byte on every field including `signals`. But `tiedCount` (→ `signals.tiedAtScore`) is computed over the passed array: in `rankDirections` over the *sliced* list (`directions.ts:1074-1081`), in `enumerateDirections` over the *full* `merged` list. When a top-score tie extends beyond `limit`, `tiedAtScore` differs between the two paths, so strict prefix identity breaks on `signals` — and Desired End State #6's full-list semantics are exactly what defeats byte-for-byte prefix identity. **Addressed post-review**: the dispatcher folded the recommended fix into Phase 1's Success Criteria (exclude `tiedAtScore` from the prefix equality or use a non-boundary-crossing fixture, plus a dedicated tie-semantics test pinning the intended divergence).
